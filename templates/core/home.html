About BIG-S

Bharat Internet Governance Sphere ‘BIG-S’ Portal is a digital platform that brings together information, discussions, and opportunities for engagement in India’s Internet governance ecosystem.

It serves as a national interface where citizens, researchers, industry, and civil society can:

  • Track policy developments.
  • Access consultations and resources.
  • Participate in shaping India’s digital future.

By consolidating policy updates, stakeholder inputs, and global engagements in one place, BIG-S makes Internet governance in India more accessible, transparent, and participatory.

The platform supports India’s vision of a secure, open, inclusive, and trusted Internet while strengthening its role in global digital governance.

An open, secure, inclusive and trusted Internet that drives innovation, economic growth and social development for all in India and beyond.

Internet Governance Landscape in India

  • India's digital transformation has created new opportunities and challenges for Internet governance, from expanding connectivity and digital public infrastructure to ensuring a safe, secure and inclusive online ecosystem.
  • The Internet and digital technologies are now central to India's social, economic and political life, making collaborative and forward-looking governance essential.
  • BIG-S provides a platform to bring together diverse stakeholders, foster dialogue, generate research and contribute to India's engagement in global internet governance processes.

Areas of Focus

Policy and Regulation

Supporting informed, inclusive and evidence-based discussions for a free, open and secure internet in India.

Multi-Stakeholder Consultations

Creating spaces for dialogue between government, industry, academia, technical community and civil society.

Global and Regional Engagement

Contributing to India's participation in global and regional internet governance processes.

Capacity Building and Awareness

Growing knowledge, skills and awareness across the internet governance community.

Emerging Technologies

Exploring governance implications of new and emerging technologies such as AI, IoT, data governance and cybersecurity.
