A link between government and stakeholders

Special Interest Groups (SIGs) connect the Government with domestic stakeholders and keep a structured two-way flow of information, insights, and feedback.

Academia, industry, civil society, and technical communities take part in policy and technical discussions, while the Government can share priorities, build consensus, and support national and global initiatives.

SIGs also strengthen capacity in technical and policy domains and support India’s leadership in global Internet Governance.

Current Special Interest Groups

SIG on Domain Names and Numbers

SIG–DNN

Focuses on policy, operational, and technical aspects related to domain names, IP addresses, and numbering resources, including global coordination mechanisms and India’s participation.

SIG on Internet Protocols and Standards

SIG–IPS

Engages stakeholders on Internet standards development, protocol evolution, and technical coordination processes that underpin a secure, stable, and interoperable Internet.

SIG on Future of Internet

SIG–FI

Addresses emerging Internet governance challenges related to future technologies, digital public infrastructure, resilience, inclusion, and long-term evolution of the Internet ecosystem.
