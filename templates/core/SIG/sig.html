Special Interest Groups (SIG)

SIGs are expert working groups that collaborate on focused Internet governance topics and contribute to consultations and events.

About Us

The Special Interest Groups (SIGs) are India’s collaborative platform for Internet governance bringing government, industry, academia, civil society, and the technical community together to shape standards, policies, and practices that serve the country’s digital future.

Guided by the Internet Governance Division, MeitY, and supported by NIXI, the SIGs convert expert dialogue into actionable outputs: position papers, policy recommendations, capacity-building programs, and inputs to global bodies such as IETF, ICANN, APNIC, and W3C.

Our SIGs

SIG — Internet Protocols & Standards

SIG — Domain Names & Related Issues

Covers domain name management, DNS policies, and related Internet infrastructure.

SIG — Future of Internet

Explores emerging technologies, governance challenges, and trends shaping the Internet.
