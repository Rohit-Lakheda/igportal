Bharat Internet Governance Sphere ‘BIG-S’ Portal is envisaged as a unified digital platform to support public engagement, information dissemination, and consultations related to India’s participation in various Internet Governance processes at both national and international levels.

The portal serves as a national interface connecting citizens, experts, institutions, and stakeholders with India’s participation in Internet Governance processes at both national and international levels.

By bringing together policy discussions, consultations, resources, and updates in one place, BIG-S portal supports India’s vision of a secure, open, inclusive, and trusted Internet aligned with national priorities and global responsibilities.

India’s Internet Governance Vision

A secure, resilient, inclusive, and trusted Internet ecosystem that supports innovation, safeguards national interests, and contributes constructively to global digital cooperation.

Internet Governance Landscape in India

Internet Governance in India is guided by a policy-led and institution-driven framework, with the Government of India playing a central role in steering the development, management, and use of the Internet in alignment with national priorities, constitutional values, and international commitments.

The Government of India is responsible for setting the policy, legal, and regulatory direction for the Internet and digital ecosystem, while engaging with relevant stakeholders as required for informed decision-making.

India participates actively in regional and international Internet Governance processes, contributing to discussions on norms, standards, and frameworks that shape the global Internet.

Areas of Focus

Policy & Regulation

Tracking and sharing updates on national and international Internet-related policies, laws, and regulatory discussions impacting India.

Multi-Stakeholder Consultations

Facilitating structured consultations to gather inputs from diverse stakeholders on Internet Governance issues and global discussion tracks.

Global & Regional Engagement

Supporting India’s engagement in international Internet Governance forums by disseminating information, timelines, and participation opportunities.

Capacity Building & Awareness

Promoting understanding of Internet Governance concepts, institutions, and processes through resources, explainers, and knowledge materials.

Emerging Technologies

Addressing governance challenges related to digital public infrastructure, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data governance, and inclusion.
