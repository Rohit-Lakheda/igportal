Proposed GNSO Process for ICANN Board to Reverse Adoption of GNSO Policy Recommendations

Public comment proceeding seeking community input on proposed updates to GNSO operating procedures.

🔔 Get alerts about this proceeding
Category: Policy
Requester(s): Generic Names Supporting Organization (GNSO)
Contact(s): policy-staff@icann.org
Status: Open for Public Comment

What We Need Your Input On

The GNSO Council is proposing a process for the ICANN community’s consideration to enable the ICANN Board to reconsider a previously adopted policy recommendation from the GNSO.

This proposal is based on the identification of a procedural gap, specifically the lack of a defined process for the Board to reconsider an adopted GNSO policy recommendation.

The proposed process would be incorporated into updates to the GNSO Operating Procedures, including revisions to the Policy Development Process (PDP) Manual and the GNSO Guidance Process (GGP) Manual.

These updates aim to balance the need for clear expectations for both the GNSO Council and the ICANN Board, while ensuring that the Board’s fiduciary duties are not constrained.

The GNSO is seeking community input to ensure that the proposed updates strike the appropriate balance between flexibility and accountability.
