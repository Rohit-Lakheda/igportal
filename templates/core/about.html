About the Internet Governance Division

The Internet Governance Division (IGD) coordinates India’s engagement on Internet governance policy, standards, and global cooperation.

Who We Are

The Internet Governance Division (IGD), under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), coordinates India’s approach to how the global Internet is governed—its rules, resources, and technical standards.

IGD brings together ministries, regulators, industry, academia, civil society, and the technical community to ensure that India’s priorities are clearly represented at national and international forums.

What We Do

  • Develop policy positions on domain names, Internet number resources, routing security, and trust & safety
  • Engage with global Internet governance and standards bodies
  • Promote secure, interoperable, and inclusive Internet infrastructure in India
  • Strengthen participation of Indian experts in technical and policy processes

How We Work

  • Policy Coordination: Multi-stakeholder consultations and consensus building
  • Technical Stewardship: Adoption of secure Internet standards and best practices
  • Institutional Support: Coordination with NIXI and Special Interest Groups (SIGs)
  • Community Engagement: Workshops, consultations, and public resources

Areas of Focus

DNS & Domain Name Policy (IDNs / UA)
Internet Number Resources & Routing Hygiene
Internet Protocols & Standards Adoption
Security & Resilience of Infrastructure
Inclusion, Accessibility & User Trust
International Cooperation

Through this work, IGD helps align India’s Internet priorities with global developments and ensures sustained technical and policy contributions from India.
